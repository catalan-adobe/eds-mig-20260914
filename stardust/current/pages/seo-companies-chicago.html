Written by Brian Dean

Last updated on Apr. 01, 2019

On this page

  • Here’s What We Did (Our Process)

If you’re looking for a list of the best SEO companies in Chicago, you’ve come to the right place.

Here’s the deal:

Most Chicago SEO agency rankings are completely subjective.

We wanted to do things differently. Specifically, we decided to rank agencies using an objective rating system.

Here’s What We Did (Our Process)

  1. Compiled a list of SEO agencies in and around Chicago
  2. Gathered a bunch of data for each one
  3. Created a scoring “algorithm” to rank the entire list

To create our scoring model, we compiled 8 pieces of data for each of the SEO agencies on our list:

  1. BBB rating
  2. # of years in business
  3. # of Google reviews
  4. Average Google rating
  5. # of Yelp reviews
  6. Average Yelp rating
  7. Domain rank
  8. Estimated monthly organic traffic

Next, we converted each metric into a number on a five point scale.

BBB Ratings converted to a 5-point scale
BBB Rating
A B C D F or N/A
5 4 3 2 1

(Go here to see detailed rankings for every company on this list).

After we converted each metric into a score from 1-5, we weighed each factor based on the parameters we believe to be most important.

So we came up with this equation:

(BBB Rating * 0.1) + (Years in Business * 0.1) + (# of Google Reviews * 0.2) + (Avg. Google Rating * 0.1) + (# of Yelp Reviews * 0.2) + (Avg. Yelp Rating * 0.1) + (Domain Rank * 0.1) + (Est. Monthly Organic Traffic * 0.1)

By no means is this a perfect model. But we think it does a solid job of ranking each agency based on objective data.

Scoring Note: Our thought process behind giving the number of reviews the most weight was that if a company has 2-3 reviews and they have 5 stars, it shouldn’t carry as much weight as another company with 10+ reviews and 5 stars. It’s easy to game 2-3 reviews but harder to get double-digit reviews that way. Also, in the event of a tie score between 2 or more agencies, we ranked those agencies based on the total number of reviews (Google + Yelp).
